Shloka Pipeline – Privacy Policy

Shloka Pipeline is a personal automation tool used only by its owner to upload Sanskrit chant videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

It uses the YouTube Data API solely to upload videos. It does not collect, store, share or sell any data about other users. The only Google account it accesses is the owner's, with the owner's consent, and access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
